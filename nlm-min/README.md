## How to ask the theory

**Full prompt guide:** [PROMPTS.md](../PROMPTS.md)

Quick examples:

### If you are a medical doctor

- *"A patient has chronic pelvic pain and no structural cause. What does the field model propose about the attractor state sustaining it?"*
- *"Which medical claims are labelled open hypothesis rather than established mechanism?"*
- *"How does the theory model allostatic load and burnout?"*

### If you are a therapist or clinician

- *"What does the soma-field model propose about why trauma forms faster than it heals?"*
- *"How does the USF model the window of tolerance?"*
- *"What is the Field-Modulated Hopfield Network, and what does it assume?"*

### If you are a physicist or mathematician

- *"What is the USF propagator, and how is it motivated by an 11-dimensional decomposition?"*
- *"Which Osterwalder–Schrader results come from OSforGFF, and what is the USF identification step?"*
- *"What do the Lean files check, and which statements depend on axioms?"*

### If you are a neuroscientist

- *"What is the Missing Limbic Layer and how does the FM-HN extend Hopfield networks?"*
- *"How does QUANT-EXP-1 simulate barrier crossing between emotional attractors?"*

### If you want to test the theory

- *"What are the five experimental benchmarks described in P12?"*
- *"What did the exact 8-qubit QUANT-EXP-1 statevector simulation show?"*
- *"What would falsify the USF theory?"*

## Tips

- **Focused question** → use one paper or one source
- **Broad overview** → ask with both sources active
- **Technical depth** → ask for equations and evidence labels
- **Clinical application** → ask what is hypothesis, case material, or established external evidence
- NotebookLM citations are aids, not validation; verify important claims against the PDF pages
